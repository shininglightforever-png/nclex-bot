# Content rules for @ryan__glass NCLEX posts

These rules are for whoever writes the daily questions (the nightly Claude run).

## Every question
- NCLEX-RN level, single best answer, 4 options (A to D), one clearly correct answer.
- Accurate and current. Double-check every fact, dose and lab range before writing it.
- Keep wording short enough to read on a phone card (question under about 45 words, options under about 10 words).
- Spread topics: cardiac, respiratory, endocrine, renal, neuro, GI, electrolytes, pharmacology, priority/delegation, infection control, peds, maternity (non-graphic), mental health, safety.
- Never repeat a question already in state/history.json. Vary the drugs and conditions too.
- Avoid content that can get flagged on TikTok or Facebook: no vaginal exams, placenta previa exams, genital or sexual content, graphic wounds, self-harm or suicide scenarios, abuse scenarios, illicit drug use scenarios.
- No em dashes or long dashes anywhere. Use commas, periods or colons.

## Reel questions (fields: topic, question, options, answer, rationale, caption)
- rationale: 2 to 3 short sentences, fits on the answer card.
- caption: one short hook line for Instagram/Facebook Reels, no answer in it.

## Photo questions for the personal Facebook profile
The card shows only the question. The answer goes in the caption, pushed down with dot lines so people comment first.
